Hey :wave:! I'm a first year CS PhD student at **Cornell Tech** :bear: :aerial_tramway:, fortunate to be supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. I am also a Guest Researcher at the **Flatiron Institute**.

I'm broadly interested in **probablistic machine learning** :robot: :

- generative models
- AI4Science
- sampling & statistical inference

My research is currently focused on **diffusion language models** that are efficient, scalable, and performant.

Previously I interned at **Uber** working on RL for experimentation.

Before grad school, I spent two wonderful years at the **Flatiron Institute** with [Dr. Bob Carpenter](https://bob-carpenter.github.io). Before that, I graduated from **Columbia** :lion: with a BA in Applied Math.
